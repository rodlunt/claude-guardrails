# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue with the idea, not the person.

This is a small, solo-maintained repository, so a long formal document would be theatre.
What follows is the whole policy.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, including when you think someone is wrong.
- Criticise code, reasoning and decisions freely. That is the point of the project.
- Accept that "no" is a normal answer, and that the maintainer's judgement decides what
  merges here.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Discrimination or slurs of any kind.
- Publishing anyone's private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or issues.

## Enforcement

Reports go to the maintainer privately, through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting
on this repository (**Security → Report a vulnerability**), which is the available
private channel and works fine for conduct matters too.

The maintainer may edit, lock or delete contributions, and block accounts, at their
discretion. There is no appeals process; this is one person's repository, not an
institution.

## Scope

Applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and commit messages here, and anywhere
someone is representing this project.
